# Adobe CX Enterprise interface and administration guide

[Adobe CX Enterprise](https://experience.adobe.com) (Customer Experience Enterprise) connects Adobe's applications for content, engagement, data, and brand visibility into agent-driven workflows, so you can orchestrate the customer experience from one integrated system.

![CX Enterprise](assets/cx-enterprise-home.png)

## What you can do with CX Enterprise {#capabilities}

CX Enterprise brings together applications for every stage of the customer experience lifecycle:

| Capability | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Orchestrate work with AI agents | Use [Adobe CX Enterprise Coworker](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/cx-enterprise-ai/experience-cloud-ai/coworker/overview) to automate customer experience and marketing workflows, with agents that act within the guardrails you set. |
| Engage customers in real time | Unify customer data and orchestrate personalized journeys with [Adobe Real-Time CDP](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/docs/experience-platform/rtcdp/overview.html) and [Adobe Journey Optimizer](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/journey-optimizer/using/get-started/get-started), and test and personalize experiences with [Adobe Target](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/docs/target.html). |
| Create and manage content at scale | Plan, produce, and govern content with [Adobe GenStudio for Performance Marketing](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/genstudio-for-performance-marketing/user-guide/get-started), [Adobe Experience Manager](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/docs/experience-manager-cloud-service.html), and [Adobe Workfront](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/workfront/using/basics/home/home). |
| Measure and optimize | Analyze experience data across applications with [Adobe Analytics](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/docs/analytics.html). |
| Sell online | Run digital commerce with [Adobe Commerce](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/docs/commerce.html). |
| Protect and grow brand visibility | Monitor and improve how your brand appears in AI-driven search and conversations with [Adobe Brand Visibility](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/brand-visibility/using/essentials/overview). |
| Manage data and governance | Collect, govern, and activate data across applications on [Adobe Experience Platform](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/docs/experience-platform.html). |

{style="table-layout:auto"}

This guide covers the shared interface and administration layer that ties these applications together, not the features of each individual application.

## The CX Enterprise interface {#interface}

From the CX Enterprise header, you can:

* [Access](get-started-using-cxe.md#applications) all your CX Enterprise applications and services
* From the **[!UICONTROL Help]** menu, search for product documentation, tutorials, and community posts. View results in Experience League.
* Globally [search](/help/interface/features/search.md) business objects using a global search (Experience Platform users only) in the Search field.
* Access [Admin Console](/help/interface/administration/admin-console.md) for user and product management
* Manage your account [preferences](features/account-preferences.md) (alerts, notifications, and subscriptions)

To sign in, launch your applications, and get familiar with the interface, see [Get started using CX Enterprise](get-started-using-cxe.md).
